## Facts used (researched July 2026) + WORDING RULES (obey strictly)

  user's phonetic "Rugionos/Rogino's."
- **Hours (consistent, no conflict):** Tue–Thu 11–8, Fri–Sat 11–9, **closed
  Sun & Mon.**
- **Signature = GRINDERS** (subs) on **homemade bread** — 21 varieties in 9" &
  18", served as a "Grinder Basket" with wedge fries; dressed with an

- **Do NOT assert a founding year** (2002 per D&B vs "~30 years" per a

  not documented) — soft "feeding a crowd? give us a call" only.
- Real review quotes available (meat lovers pizza, garlic bread, dough balls,
  roast beef on homemade bread, huge subs & wedge fries) — attribute lightly.
- Lake-country angle: Fremont is near Angola / Lake James / Pokagon State Park;

- Domains (RDAP 7/10/2026): rugionos.com, rugionospizza.com,
