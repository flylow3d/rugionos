Our claim to fame

The grinder, done right

Piled high on bread we bake in-house, dressed with an olive-oil drizzle, minced jalapeños and black olives, and served up as a Grinder Basket with a pile of wedge fries. Twenty-one varieties, in a 9-inch or a share-it 18-inch.
